How to export your Google Maps Timeline:

  1. Open Google Maps on your mobile device
  2. Tap your profile picture → Your Timeline
  3. Tap the three dots (⋮) → Settings and privacy
  4. Scroll down and tap "Export Timeline data"
  5. Select the time range and export as JSON
  6. Upload the exported file here
